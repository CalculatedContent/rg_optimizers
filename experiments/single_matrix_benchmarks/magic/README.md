# MAGIC Gamma Telescope — single-matrix benchmark

Prepared on a separate branch. Preflight is data-only. The primary workflow template is inert until installed as `.github/workflows/single-matrix-magic-primary.yml` after the Power Plant cohort terminates and its completed/partial artifacts are secured. Never replay training to recover archive files.

Data: R. Bock (2004), [MAGIC Gamma Telescope](https://doi.org/10.24432/C52C8B), UCI, CC BY 4.0. 19,020 simulated showers; 10 real-valued inputs, g=gamma (signal), h=hadron (background). No missing features. UCI reports 12,332 g and 6,688 h. UCI recommends ROC-based comparison because background rejection matters; report accuracy for ML benchmark comparison, CE, ROC-AUC, gamma acceptance at hadron false-positive rates 1%, 2%, 5%, 10%, 20%.

Reference: [Gorishniy et al., TabM, ICLR 2025](https://openreview.net/attachment?id=Sd4wYYOhmY&name=pdf), ordinary MLP 85.39% ± 0.60 pp (SD), XGBoost 85.50% ± 0.94 pp, CatBoost 85.86%, TabR 86.82% single model. Published numbers are context, not exact reproduction of the split or preprocessing. This experiment freezes a deterministic stratified five-fold group partition of official UCI rows, grouping identical feature vectors to eliminate exact duplicate feature leakage; fold 0 test, fold 1 validation, remainder train. No split selection. Compare same-split XGBoost via six validation candidates and one frozen refit.

Architecture 10 → 784 → 784 → 2 ReLU, FC2 784×784 only primary spectral observable; train-only z-score, no dropout, no weight decay for SGDM and first AdamW track; second AdamW WD=.001. SGDM LR .003/.01/.03/.10 at momentum .90 and LR .03 at momentum .80/.95. AdamW LR .0001/.0003/.001 per decay track. Two development seeds per candidate; freeze by mean fixed-20k validation accuracy, then CE and ID; five fresh confirmation seeds per track, refit train+validation, test only at step 20k. Hard cap 20k per run; 24+15=39 primary runs; maximum 780,000 neural updates, five CPU workers, 45-minute job ceiling. XGBoost separately bounded to six development fits and one refit (400 trees each) after confirmations. No optional dropout or long-horizon extension. Never select by alpha or test.

Reproduction: use Python 3.12.9 and `requirements.txt`, run `python prepare.py` only if no archived data; `python run.py` prints dry-run plan without training. `python run.py --execute-training --phase grid --case s0 --seed 3470` is one explicitly authorized case, not a smoke test. `python audit.py select` freezes complete grid; `python audit.py summarize` verifies all confirmations and XGBoost results. Archive checkpoints and full data; do not infer success from temporary artifacts alone.
